## Listings

- A **listing** is an **enhanced form of Secure Data Sharing** (same provider/consumer model). It wraps a **data product** (a **share** or a **Native App** application package) with **metadata**: title, description, sample SQL queries, provider details.
- What listings add over a direct share: **public discovery on the Marketplace**, **charging for access**, **usage metrics** for providers, rich metadata, and **cross-region / cross-cloud delivery**.

| Availability | Who sees it | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Private listing** | Only the **specific accounts** you choose | Works for accounts in **any region** |
| **Public listing** | Everyone, on the **Snowflake Marketplace** | Requires a **provider profile** |

| Access type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Free** | Instant access to the full data product |
| **Limited trial** | Instant limited access (for **1–90 days**, or a subset); consumers **request** full access |
| **Paid** | Charged through Snowflake's pricing models; requires a provider profile |

### Cross-Cloud Auto-Fulfillment

- Snowflake **automatically replicates** a listing's data product to the consumer's region when needed, so providers don't replicate data by hand.
- It's turned on automatically for private listings to accounts in other regions, and used for **paid** listings. Making a data product available in more regions brings **additional costs** for the provider.

### Providers and consumers

- **Providers** need a **full account** (not a trial) and **ACCOUNTADMIN** or a role with provider privileges. A **provider profile** is required for Marketplace or paid listings.
- **Consumers** need **ACCOUNTADMIN** or a role with **`CREATE DATABASE`** and **`IMPORT SHARE`**. In Snowsight they open the listing and click **Get** (or **Request** for trials or listings not yet in their region). A read-only database is created, just like a direct share.

## Snowflake Marketplace

- The place in Snowsight to **discover and get** third-party **data, data services and Native Apps**, and for providers to **publish** listings to all Snowflake customers.
- Available to accounts on **AWS, Azure and Google Cloud** (except Azure Government).
- Consumers get **live, ready-to-query data** with **no copies or pipelines**. They pay their own compute to query it, plus the listing price if it's paid.
- Examples: weather, financial market, demographic and geospatial data that you join with your own tables.

## Snowflake Native Apps

- The **Snowflake Native App Framework** lets providers package **data plus application logic** (stored procedures, UDFs, Snowpark code, **Streamlit** UI, and even containers via **Snowpark Container Services**) and distribute it to other accounts.
- **Provider side:** an **application package** containing a **manifest file** (configuration, versions) and a **setup script** (SQL that runs when the app is installed or upgraded). Providers release **versions and patches**.
- **Distribution:** through **listings**: free or paid, **privately** or on the **Marketplace**.
- **Consumer side:** installing the listing creates an **application object** in the **consumer's account**, and the setup script creates its objects. The app is installed and runs **in the consumer's account**. The consumer **grants the privileges** the app asks for, and can enable logging/event sharing for the provider.
- Native Apps protect the **provider's IP** (logic isn't exposed to the consumer).

> **Exam tip:** "Make data discoverable to any Snowflake customer and charge for it" → **public, paid listing on the Marketplace**. "Share with a partner account in another region" → **private listing** (auto-fulfillment). "Distribute code + data that runs in the customer's account" → **Native App**.
